Citrus Empire

Privacy Policy

Citrus Empire: Lemonade Stand, an iPhone and iPad game by Dillon Cain. Effective September 28, 2026.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your game progress stays on your device.
  • We don't sell your data, and the game doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Ads appear only when you choose to watch a short clip for a bonus. They are non-personalized and rated for all ages.
  • When you watch a clip, Google (which supplies the ads) receives some device and usage information, described below.

What stays on your device

Your stand, cash, recipes, goals and settings are saved on your device only. We don't run servers that store your game, and we can't see your progress. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders ("your lemons are waiting") are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off with the Notifications switch in the game's Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Citrus Premium, stand skins and the Citrus Cat) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game keeps a record on your device of which items you own so they carry over between updates, and Apple's receipt lets you restore them on a new device.

Reward clips and Google AdMob

The game offers optional clips in three places: doubling what your franchises earned while you were away, a bonus on the day report, and early help when you're out of supplies. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch. Citrus Premium gives these bonuses without a clip.

Clips are provided by Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalized ads only, rated G (suitable for all ages). The game does not ask for permission to track you and does not access your device's advertising identifier. To show and measure a clip, Google may collect:

DataWhy
A device identifier specific to this app, and device type and OS versionDelivering the clip and preventing fraud
Approximate location derived from your IP addressShowing ads that make sense for your country
Clip interactions (loaded, watched, tapped)Measuring ads and paying the reward
Crash and performance information from the ad softwareKeeping ads working

This data is not linked to your identity by us. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Players in the EU, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears the first time you open the game, and clips load only after you answer. You can change your choice at any time from Settings, using "Ad privacy choices".

Children

Citrus Empire is made to be fun for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: there is no sign-up, chat or sharing in the game, ads are non-personalized for every player, and only content rated for general audiences is requested.

Your choices

  • Skip clips entirely: every bonus is optional, and the game is fully playable without them.
  • Turn off reminders with the Notifications switch in the game's Settings, or in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data from your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. For questions, use the Support link on the Citrus Empire App Store page.
