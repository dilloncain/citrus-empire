Common questions

I bought something. How do I get it back on a new device?
Open the Shop, go to the Premium tab and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Citrus Premium comes back at no charge. (Stands, pets, upgrades and flavors are bought with in-game cash, not real money.)
Will my progress carry over when the game updates?
Yes. Your stand, cash and goals are kept through every update.
Can I move my progress to another device?
Progress is saved on the device you play on, so a new device starts a fresh stand. Your purchases always come with you through Restore Purchases.
What are reward clips?
Short optional videos you can choose to watch for a bonus: double what your franchises earned while you were away, a bonus on the day report, or early help when you run out of supplies. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch, and Citrus Premium gives every bonus without a clip.
I don't want reminders.
Turn off the Notifications switch in the game's Settings.
How do I start over?
In Settings, choose Start Over. Your purchases and settings are kept.
