Citrus Empire Idle

Privacy Policy

Citrus Empire Idle, a game for iPhone, iPad and Android by Dillon Cain. Effective October 6, 2026.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your game progress stays on your device.
  • We don't sell your data, and we never see your progress or purchases.
  • Ads appear only when you choose to watch a short video for a bonus. They are rated for all ages.
  • Ads are matched to your interests only if you allow it (on iPhone, when it asks; on Android, through your device’s ad settings). Your rewards are the same either way.

What stays on your device

Your stands, cash, stars, pets, styles, achievements and settings are saved on your device only. We don't run servers that store your game, and we can't see your progress. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders (for example "your free gift is ready" or "your stands are full") are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off with the Notifications switch in the game's Shop tab, or in your device’s notification settings.

Purchases

The only in-app purchase is "Remove Ads + 2× Forever", a one-time, optional upgrade. It is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and we never see your payment details. Everything else in the game is earned by playing. The store keeps a record of your purchase, so Restore Purchases brings it back on a new device signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores (franchise stars, best login streak and visitor tips) are sent to Apple's Game Center so you can see them and compare with friends. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. If you're not signed in, nothing is sent. The Android version doesn't use an achievements service: achievements stay in the game.

Bonus videos and Google AdMob

The game offers optional short videos for bonuses, such as doubling a free gift or your welcome-back earnings, 2× profit for an hour, or a Time Skip. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch, and the game is fully playable without them. "Remove Ads + 2× Forever" gives these bonuses without a video.

Videos are provided by Google AdMob, and we request only content rated G (suitable for all ages). On a later visit, the game explains and then shows Apple's tracking prompt once. If you choose Allow, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads matched to your interests. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are not personalized and the advertising identifier is not used. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, Google may use your device’s advertising ID unless you delete it or opt out of personalized ads in your device’s Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).

Players in the EU, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears when you first open the game, and ads load only after you answer. You can change your choice at any time from the Shop tab, using "Ad Privacy".

Made for all ages

Citrus Empire Idle is made to be fun for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: there is no sign-up, chat or sharing in the game, and only ads rated for general audiences are requested.

Your choices

  • Skip videos entirely: every bonus is optional.
  • Choose "Ask App Not to Track" (or turn tracking off in iOS Settings) for non-personalized ads. On Android, delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device’s ad settings.
  • Turn off reminders with the Notifications switch in the game, or in your device’s settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data from your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. For questions, email citrusempire.support@dilloncain.com.
